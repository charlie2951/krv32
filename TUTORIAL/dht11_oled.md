### Displaying Temperature and Humidity data on SSD1306 OLED Display


**Steps:**
1. Connect SSD1306 to  i2c port(select i2c0 or 1). In sample code, I2C0 is used. 
2. Connect DHT11 data line to JB pin-7 (GPIO4)
3. Build and upload the firmware <p>
**Sample demo code:**
https://github.com/charlie2951/krv32/blob/v4.0/SDK/examples/dht11_oled.c
